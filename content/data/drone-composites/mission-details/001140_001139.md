## Constituent missions

This composite mission combines two overlapping drone missions flown at different altitudes:

- [Mission 001140](/data/drone/mission-details/001140/) (higher altitude, nadir camera pitch)
- [Mission 001139](/data/drone/mission-details/001139/) (lower altitude, oblique camera pitch)
