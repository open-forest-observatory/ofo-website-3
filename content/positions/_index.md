Come work with us! We expect to announce several positions in late November 2026. The positions would sit in the [Forest Change Analysis Lab](https://focal-lab.org) (FOCAL) at UC Davis, led by Dr. Derek Young. To stay informed, join our [mailing list](/about/#mailing-list), or simply [contact us](/about/#contact-us) to express interest.

## Expected positions

### 3D forest modeling data scientist(s)

Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD-level research scientist (responsibilities tailored to expertise).

Likely areas of focus include:

- Develop tools for 3D forest reconstruction from drone and ground imagery ([summary](https://focal-lab.org/current-research/understory-forest-mapping/))
  - Translate raw imagery-based computer vision class segmentations (e.g., tree, log, shrub) onto mesh models using [Geograypher](https://github.com/open-forest-observatory/geograypher)
  - Evaluate reconstructions against ground-truth data from traditional forest inventory plots
  - Summarize semantic mesh models into forest management-relevant datasets including individual tree locations, species, and sizes
  - Evaluate potential accuracy tradeoffs of using semantic point clouds rather than semantic meshes
  - Evaluate NeRF and Gaussian splatting for under-canopy 3D reconstruction, as alternatives to traditional photogrammetry
- Develop and evaluate methods for co-registering co-located geospatial datasets from different dates and/or modalities (e.g, drone and ground-based imagery)
- Develop internal data processing pipelines for generating [OFO data products](/data/) using Argo Workflows on Kubernetes ([example](https://ofo-argo-docs.netlify.app/))
- Improve software documentation and OFO [data processing tutorials](https://docs.openforestobservatory.org/tutorials/) for the broader community
- Test and optimize computer vision-based methods for tree detection and species classification from orthomosaics, canopy height models, and raw drone imagery
- Assist in curating existing and incoming drone and ground-based datasets for inclusion in the [OFO data catalog](/data/)

### Data and field logistics manager

Duties include:

- Manage incoming drone and ground-based datasets, including metadata and data quality checks
- Coordinate field data collection and drone flights, including scheduling, permitting, and logistics
- Recruit, train, and manage field technicians
- Assist in curating existing and incoming drone, GoPro, and manual datasets for inclusion in the OFO data catalog
- Assist in developing and maintaining OFO data collection protocols and workflows
- Assist in testing and documenting OFO data processing tools and workflows

### Field crew positions

Duties include:

- Collect drone and ground-based data in the field, including piloting drones, operating GoPro cameras, and conducting traditional forest inventory surveys
- Assist in data quality checks and metadata collection
- Camp for extended periods at remote field sites, often in rugged terrain and under variable weather conditions
